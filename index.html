📚 Biotechnology Career Reference Guide

Explore core high school CTE lab skills, industry impacts, and career pathways across all 8 sector branches.

🦠

Microbial Biotechnology

Fermentation, Biomanufacturing & Microorganisms

Overview & Industry Impact

Microbial biotech utilizes bacteria, yeast, and fungi to synthesize critical human products—ranging from insulin and antibiotics to industrial bio-enzymes and sustainable biofuels.

High School CTE Lab Skills

  • 🧪 Aseptic technique and bacterial streak plating
  • 🧫 Bacterial transformation and plasmid DNA insertion
  • 📊 Bioreactor pH, temperature, and yield monitoring

💡 Target Careers

Bioprocess Technician

Fermentation Scientist

Microbiologist


💰 Salary: $55,000 - $95,000 / yr

🌾

Agricultural Biotechnology

Crop Resilience, GMOs & Food Security

Overview & Industry Impact

Agricultural biotech enhances crop yields, pest resistance, and drought tolerance through genetic engineering, molecular markers, and tissue culture—helping secure global food supply.

High School CTE Lab Skills

  • 🌱 Plant tissue micropropagation
  • 🧬 Gene editing and CRISPR sequence matching
  • 🌾 Agrobacterium-mediated gene transfer

💡 Target Careers

Plant Geneticist

Agri-Biotech Specialist

Crop Yield Analyst


💰 Salary: $60,000 - $105,000 / yr

🐾

Animal Biotechnology

Transgenic Models, Livestock & GFP Markers

Overview & Industry Impact

Animal biotech applies genetic engineering to improve livestock breeding, develop disease-resistant animals, and produce biomedical models essential for drug testing.

High School CTE Lab Skills

  • ✨ GFP (Green Fluorescent Protein) expression tracking
  • 🩺 ELISA antibody diagnostic assays
  • 🧬 Selective genomic marker analysis

💡 Target Careers

Veterinary Biologist

Transgenic Specialist

Embryology Technician


💰 Salary: $58,000 - $110,000 / yr

🔍

Forensic Biotechnology

DNA Fingerprinting & Electrophoresis

Overview & Industry Impact

Forensic biotech applies DNA profiling, STR analysis, and gel electrophoresis to solve legal cases, identify unknown samples, and support law enforcement.

High School CTE Lab Skills

  • 🧬 PCR DNA amplification
  • ⚡ Agarose gel electrophoresis loading & running
  • 📊 DNA ladder band matching and profile analysis

💡 Target Careers

Forensic DNA Analyst

Crime Lab Technician

Molecular Analyst


💰 Salary: $52,000 - $92,000 / yr

🌱

Bioremediation Biotechnology

Environmental Cleanup & Phytoremediation

Overview & Industry Impact

Bioremediation leverages living organisms—like oil-degrading bacteria or heavy-metal absorbing plants—to clean up polluted marine ecosystems and contaminated soil safely.

High School CTE Lab Skills

  • 🧫 Hydrocarbon-degrading microbe inoculation
  • 🧪 Soil biostimulation and bioaugmentation
  • 🌾 Phytoremediation tracking in aquatic plant beds

💡 Target Careers

Environmental Biotechnologist

Eco-Restoration Consultant

Water Quality Scientist


💰 Salary: $58,000 - $98,000 / yr

🌊

Aquatic Biotechnology

Marine Drugs, Aquaculture & Bio-compounds

Overview & Industry Impact

Aquatic biotech explores marine organisms and microalgae to discover novel pharmaceuticals, bio-plastics, and sustainable aquaculture solutions.

High School CTE Lab Skills

  • 🧪 Microalgae photobioreactor maintenance
  • ⚓ Marine enzyme extraction
  • 🐟 Diagnostic assays in aquaculture disease management

💡 Target Careers

Marine Biotechnologist

Aquaculture Manager

Algal Bioprocess Specialist


💰 Salary: $54,000 - $96,000 / yr

🏥

Medical Biotechnology

Therapeutics, Vaccines & Research

Overview & Industry Impact

Medical biotech leads healthcare innovation by developing mRNA vaccines, monoclonal antibodies, gene therapies, and rapid diagnostic kits.

High School CTE Lab Skills

  • 💊 Antibiotic Zone of Inhibition assays
  • 🔬 Cell culture maintenance & sterile technique
  • 🩸 Diagnostic immunoassay interpretation

💡 Target Careers

Biomedical Scientist

Clinical Research Coordinator

Pharmaceutical Developer


💰 Salary: $68,000 - $130,000 / yr

📋

Regulatory Biotechnology

FDA Compliance, QA/QC & Bioethics

Overview & Industry Impact

Regulatory biotech ensures all drugs, medical devices, and agricultural products are safe and ethically manufactured according to FDA, EPA, and USDA guidelines.

High School CTE Lab Skills

  • 📋 Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) documentation
  • 🔬 Quality Assurance and Quality Control (QA/QC) verification
  • ⚖️ Bioethics case studies & FDA submission protocols

💡 Target Careers

Regulatory Affairs Specialist

QA/QC Manager

Bioethics Consultant


💰 Salary: $70,000 - $135,000 / yr

🏫 Top New Jersey Colleges for Biotech

Explore tuition rates, campus locations, and biotech program highlights in New Jersey.

Research University

Rutgers University

📍 New Brunswick, Newark & Camden, NJ

In-State Tuition: $18,000 - $19,400 / yr
Out-of-State Tuition: $37,000 - $41,000 / yr

Rutgers has an exceptional biotechnology program that teaches important skills like proficiency in using biotechnology tools, reading scientific journals, and working as a team in a laboratory setting.

Best In-State Value

William Paterson University

📍 Wayne, NJ

In-State Tuition: $8,379 / yr
Out-of-State Tuition: $13,692 / yr

At William Paterson University you can complete a 30-credit biotechnology sequence that lays a foundation for any biotech career. You will learn skills such as gene expression, cell and tissue culture, and recombinant DNA technology.

Industry Network

Kean University

📍 Union, NJ

In-State Tuition: $16,370 / yr
Out-of-State Tuition: $16,370 / yr

Kean University offers an outstanding biotechnology program focusing on antibiotic resistance, startup founder interactions, and internship opportunities with over 100 research facilities.

Biomedical Focus

Saint Peter's University

📍 Jersey City, NJ

In-State Tuition: $41,770 / yr
Out-of-State Tuition: $41,770 / yr

Saint Peter's focuses on biomedical applications of biotechnology with multi-science coursework and internships at notable medical institutions.
